Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Something else on your mind? Contact support.

Is WatchHub free?
Yes. You can use WatchHub's core tracking features for free. The free version includes ads and up to 5 community comments per week. WatchHub Plus removes ads and unlocks unlimited community access.
What can WatchHub track?
WatchHub brings TV shows, movies and anime into one library. For episodic titles, you can follow progress episode by episode and season by season.
Can I import my TV Time history?
If you have a saved TV Time export, WatchHub can match supported titles and watch progress so you can continue without rebuilding everything manually.
Does WatchHub have community features?
Yes. WatchHub includes groups, posts, replies, reactions and GIF comments so tracking and discussion can live in the same app.
Where can I download WatchHub?
WatchHub is available now on Google Play for Android. An iOS version is coming soon.
