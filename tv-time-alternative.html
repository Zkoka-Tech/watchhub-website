What you can do

A TV show, movie and anime tracker built around progress

If you want a TV Time alternative, the most important thing is being able to open the app and immediately know what you watched, where you stopped and what comes next. WatchHub is built around that workflow.

Track episodes and seasons

Mark episodes and seasons as watched, view your progress season by season and quickly see where you left off. Movies, TV shows and anime stay together in the same library instead of being split across separate trackers.

Keep your watchlist organized

Separate titles you are watching, planning to watch, finished or stopped so your library remains useful even as it grows.

Bring supported TV Time history with you

Find what comes next

WatchHub shows upcoming episodes for the series you follow and can remind you when a new episode is released.

Talk about what you watch

WatchHub includes groups, posts, comments and replies so tracking and discussion can happen in the same app. Join conversations with people watching the same kinds of shows, movies and anime.

Free to start, with WatchHub Plus available

The core WatchHub tracker is free and supported by ads. WatchHub Plus removes ads and unlocks unlimited community participation. Monthly, yearly and lifetime options may be available through Google Play, with pricing shown in the app.

Quick checklist

What to look for in a TV Time alternative

  • Episode-by-episode and season-by-season tracking
  • Movies, TV shows and anime in one library
  • A clear watchlist with useful status categories
  • A way to bring supported existing watch history with you
  • Upcoming episode information and reminders
  • Community features if you want to discuss what you watch

WatchHub is designed around all of these needs while keeping the tracking experience simple.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I import my TV Time history into WatchHub?

If you have a saved TV Time export, WatchHub can match supported titles and watch progress. You can review the matched titles before importing them.

Does WatchHub track episodes and seasons?

Yes. You can mark episodes and seasons as watched and see your progress season by season.

Is WatchHub free?

Yes. WatchHub has a free version supported by ads. WatchHub Plus removes ads and unlocks unlimited community participation.

Is WatchHub available on iPhone?

WatchHub is currently available on Android through Google Play. An iOS version is planned for later.
