Community Guidelines

WatchHub is for people who enjoy talking about what they watch. These rules help keep groups, posts and comments useful and welcoming.

Be respectful

Disagreement is fine. Harassment, threats, targeted abuse, hateful conduct and bullying are not. Criticize the idea or the show, not the person.

Handle spoilers responsibly

Use spoiler warnings, group rules or clear context when discussing major plot details that other members may not have reached yet. Do not intentionally spoil content for other users.

Keep discussions relevant

Use groups and threads for genuine entertainment discussion. Repetitive spam, scams, misleading promotions and disruptive posting may be removed.

Respect privacy

Do not post another person's private or sensitive information without permission. Do not impersonate other people or organizations.

Respect intellectual property

Share only content you have the right to post. Do not use WatchHub to distribute pirated movies, episodes, unauthorized streams or links intended to facilitate copyright infringement.

No illegal or dangerous content

Content that facilitates illegal activity, credible threats, exploitation or other unlawful conduct is not permitted.

Moderation

We may remove content, limit community features or restrict accounts when these guidelines are violated or when action is needed to protect the community or comply with law. Context and severity may affect how moderation is applied.

Questions or reports

For support or questions about these guidelines, contact zkokatech@gmail.com.
